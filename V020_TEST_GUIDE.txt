Compare baseline vs experimental on fresh scrap sheets. For each cell,
note outward flaps, corner rounding, closure, and any unintended entry cuts.
The star shapes have five convex outer tips separated by concave notches.
For a strictly convex polygon instead of a five-point star, geometry would
need to be changed; this is the conventional star silhouette.

The model's X/Y rotation response and residual offsets are adjustable.
Do not interpret the defaults as experimentally calibrated.

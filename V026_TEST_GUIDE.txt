V0.26: SIGNED APPROACH-HISTORY GAP PROBE (NOT A VALIDATED FIX)

Purpose: distinguish whether a smooth approach-history Y correction can reduce
~0.45 mm vertical gaps in the physical cuts in cases 4 and 6.

Important: The physical sign of the residual has not been calibrated. Both
polarities are experimental, and v0.23 previously worsened the gap. Do NOT
assume either candidate is a fix. The algorithm still has unverified physics.

The included negative_gap_only and positive_gap_only directories contain
4-path patterns extracted from the same original cases 4 and 6. They are
positioned in their original grid locations, so DO NOT cut both on the same
material placement without moving to fresh material. Use the full-grid files
only if necessary. Geometry scaling defaults to 1.0 and machine X/Y
calibration remains untouched.

Generate again:
  py smart3_v026_grid.py --gap-only --y-history-mm=-0.225 --output-dir negative_gap_only
  py smart3_v026_grid.py --gap-only --y-history-mm=0.225 --output-dir positive_gap_only

Dry run each before live movement:
  py smart3_cut.py negative_gap_only/v026_grid_experimental.svg --dry-run
  py smart3_cut.py positive_gap_only/v026_grid_experimental.svg --dry-run

Live cutting requires deliberate material/bounds checks and the original
safety confirmation gate. Never cut both on top of each other.

Measure the signed vertical gap for case 4 and case 6 for EACH polarity.
Record which cut is above/below, not just absolute separation. If neither
improves, revert to v0.24 and do not tune the amplitude upward.

Model notes: y_state integrates signed Y travel with a finite bound and holds
on horizontal segments. Smooth near-horizontal gating avoids applying the
full bias on vertical and diagonal motion. The inverse solver remains the
stable v0.24/v0.25 version. This is a controlled polarity experiment, not
proof that the forward model matches the cutter.

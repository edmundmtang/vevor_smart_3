v0.25 — PRE-COMPENSATION GEOMETRY SCALING (EXPERIMENTAL)

Goals:
  1. Preserve the stable v0.24 blade-tip inverse and loop behavior.
  2. Allow optional independent X/Y dimensional adjustment of the INTENDED paths.
  3. Scale each entire test cell about its own bounding-box center; preserve placement,
     coincident vertices, and closed path endpoints.
  4. Compensate blade-tip tracking AFTER geometry scaling, never scale compensated
     holder motion or modify settings.txt machine calibration.

No dimensional adjustment (recommended first):
    py smart3_v025_grid.py

Trial based on the one measured 16-mm square (X 15.65, Y 15.47):
    py smart3_v025_grid.py --trial-square-dimension-factors

Equivalent explicit controls:
    py smart3_v025_grid.py --geometry-scale-x 1.0223642173 --geometry-scale-y 1.034259858

NOTE: These are provisional proportional corrections from a single square. If the
error is additive, they will not generalize to other dimensions. They are NOT
new X/Y motor calibration values. Default factors remain 1.0.

Before any live cut:
    py smart3_cut.py v025_grid_experimental.svg --dry-run

Only with material placement and bounds verified, on scrap:
    py smart3_cut.py v025_grid_experimental.svg --cut --confirm-live-test I_ACCEPT_MOVEMENT

Keep the main cutter's independent --blade-offset-mm and --loop-overlap-mm at 0.
Do not feed the compensated SVG back through the grid generator. Each generator
run overwrites its generated SVGs. The experimental inverse may move off-contour.

Record: square X/Y sizes, closure quality, cases 4/6 gap, star/circle wobble.
Scaling may alter closure and gap even though compensation is applied afterward;
verify empirically. This release does NOT claim to fix the ~0.45 mm gap.

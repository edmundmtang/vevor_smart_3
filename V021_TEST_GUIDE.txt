v0.21 compact test instructions

1. Keep force 200, speed 100 and current settings.txt calibration.
2. Run: py smart3_v021_grid.py
3. Run: py smart3_cut.py v021_grid_experimental.svg --dry-run
4. Inspect bounds and placement on sacrificial vinyl. Be aware that the open
   L/V tests have conditioning leads outside their desired geometry.
5. Run live only after checks:
   py smart3_cut.py v021_grid_experimental.svg --cut --confirm-live-test I_ACCEPT_MOVEMENT
6. Inspect cells 1-4 for rounding vs flaps; cell 6 for central gap (previous
   observation ~0.45 mm); cell 7 for circularity; cells 8-9 for tip/notch
   symmetry. Compare to your previous v0.20 test, not necessarily a new baseline.

This version is not physically validated. The revised model does not prove
that the actual blade-tip position has been inferred.

v0.29 — isolate history from holder apex movement; localized corner-exit trial

Physical observations motivating this test:
* Case 4: no horizontal endpoint gap, right stroke about 0.30 mm ABOVE left.
* Case 6: 0.20 mm apex gets cuts to meet, but a small flap and ~0.5 mm
  outgoing recovery remain. These are physical observations, not solved defects.

This is a controlled experimental test, NOT a validated physical fix.
All patterns are cases 4 and 6 only. Nominal geometry scale X=Y=1.0.
The Y-history amplitude stays at -0.300 mm; blade model, inverse iteration,
corner-apex magnitude 0.20 mm, machine calibration, and USB driver unchanged.

A_v028_reference: v0.28-style apex target and holder-derived Y history.
B_isolated_history: same apex target, but Y-history input is sampled from
    intended geometry rather than the modified holder path.
C_isolated_tapered: B plus smooth finite-support apex target deformation
    over 0.50 mm on both sides of the corner; 0.20 mm peak at apex.

IMPORTANT: The new taper changes the intended solver target locally, not
just the holder. It may increase overlap or create a flap. Never assume
simulated improvement implies real improvement.

Start with B (A already tested in v0.28). If case 4 improves without case 6
worsening, try C on a fresh sheet. If B makes case 4 worse, stop and report.

Dry run from this folder:
  py smart3_cut.py B_isolated_history/v029_grid_experimental.svg --dry-run
  py smart3_cut.py C_isolated_tapered/v029_grid_experimental.svg --dry-run

Live cut only after verifying machine origin, material, dry-run bounds and
safe clearance. One 140 x 125 mm vinyl piece per trial, subject to actual
bounds and origin. The SVG canvas is 124 x 111 mm.
  py smart3_cut.py B_isolated_history/v029_grid_experimental.svg --cut --confirm-live-test I_ACCEPT_MOVEMENT
  py smart3_cut.py C_isolated_tapered/v029_grid_experimental.svg --cut --confirm-live-test I_ACCEPT_MOVEMENT

Record for EACH case and variant:
  Case 4: signed vertical offset (right ABOVE left = positive), and
          whether the horizontal ends touch or overlap.
  Case 6: whether cuts meet at apex, whether flap remains, and outgoing
          recovery length. Look for overshoot or corner collision.

Experimental Y-history and apex options are OFF by default in the Model.
These generated SVGs are already compensated; do NOT compensate them again
or add blade-offset/loop-overlap in smart3_cut.py.
